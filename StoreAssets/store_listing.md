## Full description (max 4000)

```
ONE TAP. ONE BLOCK. ONE MORE RUN.

Your hero never stops running, and there's no jump button. Every tap drops a block exactly where you need it: bridge a gap, climb a wall, cover up spikes or squash an enemy. Run out of blocks at the wrong moment and it's game over. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

■ SIMPLE ONE-TAP CONTROLS
Tap anywhere to place a block. That's it. Timing and planning do the rest.

■ THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Endless Run: go as far as you can, with the world speeding up as you go.
• One Block Challenge: start with just 3 blocks and earn one every 100 m.
• Daily Challenge: the same level for every player today. Keep your daily streak alive for bigger bonuses.

■ 8 WORLDS, EACH WITH ITS OWN DANGERS
Race through Grassland, Desert, Snow, Volcano and Space, then unlock the Candy Land, Deep Ocean and Neon City bundles. Every world has its own hazards and enemies: mud pits with snakes, lava pools, ice spikes, candy corn, sea urchins, electric zappers and more.

■ UNLOCK A WHOLE COLLECTION
Collect coins on every run and spend them on heroes (Ninja, Astronaut, Robot, Ghost Wizard...), block skins, colourful trails and death effects. Themed bundles come with a hero, block, trail and effect to match.

■ MISSIONS AND LEVELS
Complete missions for XP, level up for coin rewards and track your records on the stats screen.

■ AN ORIGINAL SOUNDTRACK
9 music tracks that build as you run faster, plus unique sounds for every block, enemy and effect. Pick your favourite track in Settings.

■ PLAY ANYWHERE
Short runs, no account needed and no internet required to play.

One Block is free to play and supported by optional ads. Watch an ad to revive, double your coins or unlock ad bundles; it's always your choice.
```
